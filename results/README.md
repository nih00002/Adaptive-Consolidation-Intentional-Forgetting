# Selected Experimental Results

This folder contains representative figures from the CpE 620 Spring 2026 final project, **Adaptive Consolidation with Intentional Forgetting for Task-Aware Continual Learning**.

The figures were extracted from the saved outputs of the original project notebooks. The experiments were not rerun or modified for this portfolio repository.

## Included Results

- `mnist_proposed_accuracy_trajectory.png` — Proposed-method task accuracy trajectory on the two-task MNIST proof of concept
- `cifar100_final_average_accuracy.png` — Split CIFAR-100 final average accuracy comparison
- `cifar100_mean_forgetting.png` — Split CIFAR-100 mean forgetting comparison
- `core50_final_average_accuracy.png` — CORe50 New Classes final average accuracy comparison
- `core50_mean_seen_accuracy_trajectory.png` — Mean accuracy over seen tasks across the seven-task CORe50 sequence
- `core50_reordered_mean_forgetting.png` — Mean forgetting under the fixed permuted CORe50 task order
